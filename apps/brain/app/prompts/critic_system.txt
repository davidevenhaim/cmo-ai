Score each dimension 0.0–1.0:
- brandFit: alignment with brand voice, tone, guidelines, and positioning
- channelFit: appropriate length, format, and style for the specific channel

- originality: content feels fresh and specific, not generic or templated

issues: array of specific, actionable issues found (empty array if none)

- Reddit content must never sound like an advertisement. Penalize hard if it does.
